# build-intl.mjs: the international side of the ledger

Per-player senior "A" international appearances and goals. RSSSF's cap logs give the cap list, the FIFA/UEFA archive and ESPN add lineup detail and act as a second source, Wikidata's lineups and goals (CC0) are a third lineup source and the only CC0 one, and martj42 is the match spine and the competitive-goal check. The plan behind it is `research/internationals.md`; the gap sizing is `research/intl-gap.md` §4-6.

```sh
node pipeline/build-intl.mjs            # build data/ledger/intl/* (~15 s, ~3 GB heap; add --max-old-space-size=8000 if node runs short)
node pipeline/build-intl.mjs --gate     # build, then the known-answer gates -> gates.json (exit 1 on a fail)
node pipeline/build-intl.mjs --review   # build, then print the identity review listing
```

It reads only files on disk and never fetches. Rerun it whenever the archive harvest, the RSSSF parse, the ESPN cache or `data/corrections/intl.json` changes; every output is rebuilt from scratch.

Code: `pipeline/build-intl.mjs` (CLI, outputs), `pipeline/lib/intl/` (`io.mjs` paths and readers, `spine.mjs` martj42 and joins, `sources.mjs` archive/ESPN adapters, `wikidata.mjs` the Wikidata adapter and the archive gate's name rules, `names.mjs` name rules, `identity.mjs` assignment and union-find, `ledger.mjs` rows and conflicts, `corrections.mjs`, `gates.mjs`). It imports `canonTeam` from `pipeline/lib/archive/gates.mjs` (the harvester's team alias map) and extends it locally; it never edits the harvester or the club ledger.

## The spine and the joins

Wikidata matches join through the harvester's own martj42 join (`matches.json` `martj42`), else the same lookup; 6,728 of 6,790 join (the 62 misses are regional sides, CIS 1992 and placeholder dates). Every source joins martj42 on the two teams (canonical names: the archive alias map plus `LOCAL` in `spine.mjs` for Czechia, Türkiye, Korea Republic, ...) and the date within ±2 days, nearest first. Undated archive records (some FIFA 1998 CONCACAF pages) and dated ones more than 2 days off (FIFA's Costa Rica 3-0 Trinidad and Tobago is 2001-03-28, martj42 2001-03-25) join on the two teams and the score inside the campaign window, unique or nothing. Every non-zero offset is a `date_disagreement` line in `join-misses.jsonl`; ESPN's -1 offsets for evening kickoffs in the Americas are labelled as UTC dates. RSSSF caps use the parser's own join (`join.opponent_match`: name, date-typo-suspected, weak), else the same lookup.

## Identity

RSSSF writes full names ("David Robert Joseph Beckham", "Pedro Miguel Carreiro Resendes 'Pauleta'"), FIFA writes `SURNAME Given` or the known-as name (`OWEN Michael`, `CRISTIANO RONALDO`), UEFA 2000 writes "Michael Owen", UEFA 2002-03 writes surnames, ESPN has athlete ids and display names, martj42 has one scorer spelling per player.

**Name rules** (`names.mjs`): fold to ASCII (NFKD, which also splits RSSSF's Dutch ligature in "Hooĳdonk", plus ø ß æ œ đ ł ı þ ð); lowercase; drop apostrophes inside words; keep quoted nicknames as tokens; ignore particles (de, da, dos, van, der, von, di, del, al, bin, ...) and token order; tokens match one-to-one (so "Gurban Gurbanov" doesn't match "Makhmud Gurbanov"), exactly, by a 4+-letter prefix, or by a transliteration key (ks/x, y/i, h/g, w/v, ph/f, kh/h, c/k, z/s, oo/ou/u, doubled letters) within one edit, a transposition counting as one (two edits from 9 letters): Husyn = GUSIN, Georgalides = GIORGALLIDES, Aleksei = Alexei, Seok-Joo = Seok-ju. Score 1 when every token of the shorter form is found and there are 2+, 0.75 for a single known-as token (DECO, RONALDO), less for partial; ties go to the form with fewer unmatched tokens, and an exact match on the quoted nickname wins (ESPN "Ronaldinho" takes RSSSF "Ronaldo de Assis Moreira 'Ronaldinho'" before ESPN "Ronaldo" can compete for it).

**Stage 1, per team-match** (same martj42 match, same side): archive and ESPN entries that appeared are assigned to that team-match's RSSSF caps, greedily from the best score, with ties refused (both stay unassigned and go to the review list). A second pass pairs leftovers that share one 4+-letter token, unique both ways, preferring a shared surname (FIFA's capitals or RSSSF's last token): "Jay Jay OKOCHA" to "Augustine Okocha". A cap left over may then match an unused substitute (a `rsssf_cap_listed_unused` conflict). Archive and ESPN leftovers pair with each other (a shared shirt number adds 0.15). martj42 scorers go to the slot the sources say scored, then any slot, then by shared token, then by a unique equal goal count ("Tostão" to "Eduardo Gonçalves de Andrade"). If any martj42 scorer stays unassigned, martj42 can't say 0 for anyone else on that side. Each result is a slot: one person in one team-match.

**Wikidata, per team-match** (`wikidata.mjs` `attachWikidata`): the side's P710 participants are paired with that team-match's slots by the rules `gate-wikidata.mjs` uses (ported, since the gate script runs on import): every name a slot holds (RSSSF, archive, ESPN, martj42) against the item's label, 16 language labels and aliases, token sets after folding (exact set 4; one side's tokens all in the other's 3; fuzzy tokens 2; a family-name token with the same shirt 1), x10 plus 3 for the same shirt, best first, one to one; then the loose pass, transliteration-folded surname similarity of 0.7 or more (+0.15 same shirt) with the given-name veto, marked `probable`. The one deliberate difference: names fold with NFKD (the RSSSF ligature rule above), not NFD. A leftover participant whose surname a leftover slot carries under another given name is a namesake (Wikidata linked the wrong item: FIFA's Robert Popov, Wikidata's Goran Popov) and gets no identity edge; other leftovers become slots of their own. Edge weights: exact 1, fuzzy 0.9, family+shirt 0.8, loose 0.6 (so one loose vote never merges a QID into a person; two do).

**Stage 2, union-find** over forms (`r:<slug>`, `a:<family>:<team>:<name key>`, `e:<athlete id>`, `g:<team>:<scorer key>`, `w:<QID>`; one QID is one person in every match), edges from the slots plus "same 2+-token spelling, same team, another archive cycle", strongest first (votes, then mean score). A person holds at most one RSSSF log, and two forms of the same source in the same team-match are different people; an edge that would break either is refused and listed. A single vote below 0.75 never merges.

**Keys**: `ip:<rsssf slug>` for a logged player (`ip:cronaldo-intlg`); `ip:<team>:<name>:<first year>` for one known only from the archive, ESPN or martj42 (`ip:portugal:maniche:2003`).

**Confidence**: for a form on a logged person, votes / team-matches the form and the log share. `identity-review.json` lists forms below 0.8 or linked only by the weak passes, refused merges, refused ties, split slots (a slot whose forms ended in different people; the RSSSF form decides), and pairs of RSSSF logs whose names are equal in shared team-matches (possible duplicate logs: `kuw-ffadhli-intl` + `kuw-shaheen-intl`, `momar-intlg` + `vae-shaddadi-intlg`).

**Join to the club ledger (proposed, not done)**: the club ledger keys on ESPN athlete ids, and so does every ESPN international summary, so `players.json` carries `espn_athlete_ids` wherever a cached ESPN international gives one (Ronaldo 22774, Messi 45843, Gerrard 7935). Otherwise join on name + country + birth year; no birth-year source is on disk (RSSSF logs and the archive have none), so that part waits for one. Do the join once both ledgers exist, through a merge table, never on name alone.

| Field | Order | When sources disagree |
|---|---|---|

A source appearance in a match his log doesn't list is an `appearance_not_in_rsssf` conflict (not a row) inside the log's span, and a row flagged `beyond_rsssf_log` after its last cap (the 1-5-cap lag of active players). A cap the archive or ESPN lineup doesn't show is `rsssf_cap_not_in_lineup` (only where that source has the side's full lineup; FIFA 1998 sides with unrecorded sub minutes are skipped) or `rsssf_cap_listed_unused` |
| started, on/off minutes, minutes | archive = ESPN > Wikidata (P793 sub and card events; 660 matches) > RSSSF (Czech logs) | the archive is taken when both have it; `started` or minutes more than 2 apart are conflicts. A FIFA 1998 substitute with no minutes counts as appeared when Wikidata lists him (`appeared_from_wikidata`); Wikidata listing an archive or ESPN unused substitute is `wikidata_lists_unused_sub` (no cap) |
| goal minutes | archive > ESPN > Wikidata, after the clock rules below | a gap left after the rules is a `goal_minutes` conflict with all three values; martj42 decides where it equals exactly one (M42-VOTE) |
| goals | RSSSF > archive > ESPN > Wikidata > martj42 | an archive or Wikidata 0 counts only where that source's goal list for the side adds up to the side's score (ARCH-COMPLETE; Wikidata's P1363 `P518` is the side credited, own goals included); | any two sources apart is a `goals` conflict; in competitive matches the pick must equal martj42 where martj42 lists every goal for that side, or it is `goals_vs_martj42`; a martj42 goal for a logged player in a match his log lacks is `martj42_goal_without_rsssf_cap` |

Minutes: a starter plays from 0, a substitute from his on-minute; to his off-minute (substitution or red card) or 90 (120 after extra time). ESPN on/off come from the substitution key events (the first participant comes on, deduped by the pair) and red-card plays; ESPN goals from scoring key events, own goals apart, near-duplicates (same scorer within 2') dropped while a side has more goals than its score.

Players with no RSSSF log get rows from archive and ESPN appearances, plus martj42 scorer rows once the person has an archive or ESPN form (a scorer appeared). They are `intl_coverage: partial` with counts and a `badge` string, so the site shows "internationals not yet covered" rather than a silently short curve. Vardy and Lacazette have nothing cached at all and are explicit `none` stubs.

## Wikidata: the clock and goal rules, and what the archive gate's disagreements turned out to be

Wikidata's P1390 is minutes into the period (P6887). First half +N is N', second half +N is 45+N', first period of extra time +N is 90+N', second period +N is 105+N'; a value past the period's length is stoppage (first half +47 is 45+2, second half +48 is 90+3, extra time over 15 runs on from 105 or 120). Rules, all in `sources.mjs` / `wikidata.mjs` / `ledger.mjs`:

- **STOP**: two clocks agree when the capped minute is the same, however the stoppage is written (archive 90 v Wikidata 90+3, archive 90+3 v Wikidata "second half +45"); the form that states the stoppage is kept. 16 of the gate's 215 goal-minute disagreements are only this (Šuker 90 v +48, Croatia 1-0 Ireland 1999; Baltusnikas 90+3 v +45).
- **TOL**: within one minute agrees. Over every paired goal Wikidata runs a little high, not 1-2 minutes high: +1 is 2-4x as common as -1 in euro2000 and fifa1998 (fifa1998 second halves 83 v 22, mean +0.27), flat in fifa2010 (mean +0.01), and 2+ gaps are rare and near symmetric (euro2000: 59 at +2..+10, 54 at -2..-8 across all sources). So Zidane 66 v 68 (Armenia 2-3 France, UEFA 54964, martj42 67) is a 2-minute disagreement, not a convention; no shift is applied.
- **ARCH-STOP**: FIFA 2002/2006 write first-half stoppage as the running minute with a plus ("47+'" = 45+2; Gerrard's Munich goal is "48+'"). Only 46+ to 50+: of 25 such goals martj42 has 45 for most, while "54+'" (Keane, Ireland v Cyprus 2004, martj42 54), "55+'" and "78+'" (Sneijder, martj42 78) are plain minutes.
- **WD-CLOCK**: a second-half P1390 of 60 or more is a match-clock minute entered in the wrong form (11 goals: Füllkrug "+68" in Germany 5-1 Scotland 2024, Q123597080, scored at 68'; De Bruyne "+80", Q123597103). Values up to 59 are stoppage (Taremi "+58" is 90+13, England 6-2 Iran 2022).
- **WD-DUP**: a side whose P1363 statements outnumber its score may hold one goal twice, once per notation (De Bruyne "+80" and "+34" in Belgium 2-0 Romania 2024); a player's second statement within 2 minutes is dropped while the side is over its score.
- **ARCH-OG**: an archive goal is an own goal when its line says "( og )" or its side lists more goals than it scored while the other side lists fewer (FIFA's Karagounis (GRE) 48' in Albania 2-0 Greece 2000; Abdul Ridha (IRQ) 56' in Kazakhstan 3-1 Iraq 1997). The harvester's own re-parse of 2026-10-22 00:33-00:39Z already types the UEFA Euro 2000 own goals (Kovtun "87 ( og )", Haas, Wetl, Govedarica), which the gate CSV of 00:23Z still counted as scorer disagreements; the rule now catches the 2 FIFA ones left.
- **ARCH-COMPLETE**: FIFA and UEFA file own goals under the side credited (152 of 152), and a side's goal lines add up to its score in all but 15 sides. In those 15 (UEFA's Euro 2004 qualifying index pages list 2 of San Marino 0-5 Hungary's goals and none of Northern Ireland 0-1 Armenia's), a player's archive 0 is unknown, not 0.
- **M42-VOTE**: where the archive (or ESPN) and Wikidata still differ, martj42's minute decides when it equals exactly one of them; otherwise the archive stands. The conflict is written either way, with all three values. martj42 is not a copy of Wikidata: on the gate's 160 decisive goal-minute gaps it sides with the archive 106 times and Wikidata 54 (Euro 2000 is the exception, 19 to 8 for Wikidata: UEFA's history pages are the weaker source for minutes).

## Corrections

`data/corrections/intl.json`, applied last over the merged rows; sources are never edited. Kind `reassign_lineup_entry`: a source lineup entry belongs to another player. The entry's start and minutes move to the target's row, `set` overrides fields, the row it gave the wrong player is removed (or loses that source), and conflicts for either player in that match get `resolved_by`. Every receipt with a `cache` path is string-checked against the cached page on each build; an RSSSF receipt is checked against the cap's `line_quote`. The correction report is in `build-summary.json` and the `corrections_applied` gate.

Kind `goal_minute`: one source has a goal at the wrong minute; `{player_key, wrong_source, wrong_value, right_value}` puts `right_value` in the row's `goal_minutes` and resolves the player's `goal_minutes` conflict in that match. Applied only when a receipt verifies. A Wikidata receipt names the cached SPARQL result and a binding in it (`statement`, `binding: {pq: 'P1390', qv: '33'}`), checked structurally on every build; an archive receipt quotes the goal line as the cached page has it.

## RSSSF goals rule (2026-10-22)

`parse-rsssf.mjs` used to let RSSSF's running total override the per-match goals column whenever the next row built on it, and read a lone running-total token as goals scored. That gave Mbappé 2 v Gibraltar (2023-06-16) where the page's goals column says 1 (`" 69   1\t40"`; RSSSF's running total is out of step for caps 67-70). Now, in logs with a match-goals column:

- The match-goals column is authoritative. A running total that disagrees with previous total + match goals adds `cumulative_mismatch` to the row (`parse_warning`) and to the log's warnings, and the check resyncs to the page's total.
- A lone token is placed by its column (tab-expanded end position against the log's two-token rows). In the running-total column it means a blank goals cell, read as 0 (Villa v Latvia: `"22      10"` after `"1  10"`). In the goals column it means a blank running total, read as goals (Kadir, Indonesia: `"  1   1       11- 8-68"`).
- Logs whose only goals column is a running total (no per-match column) still take each match's goals from the rise. A blank row there is `null`, not 0. Logs with no goals column stay `null`.

Effect on all 4,702 logs: 71 rows' `player_goals` changed in 57 logs (no other field changed); 138 rows in 83 logs carry `cumulative_mismatch`; logs whose goals equal the stated goals total went from 1,113 to 1,127 of 2,771 (25 gained, 11 lost). The caps gate is unchanged at 4,618 of 4,682 (98.6%). Where martj42 can check a blank goals cell under a rising running total, it confirms no goal in 41 of 43 cases: RSSSF usually bumps the total a row early and the next row carries the goal (Messi 110/111, Mkhitaryan 77/78, Džeko 68/69, Mitrović 103/104). The exceptions are Griezmann v Netherlands 2023 (`intl-0049`) and É. Méndez v Chile 2004. Known costs: Son v Colombia 2017 friendly (page "1  20" after 18, he scored twice) is now 1, with no second source on disk; Kadir's B-team table is still misread as caps (repeated cap numbers, a separate table-kind bug), so his total moved from 72 to 76 against 70 stated.

Gates after the rule and `intl-0048`/`intl-0049` (`node pipeline/build-intl.mjs --gate`): **11 pass, 2 fail, 1 pending.** Mbappé now equals martj42 (55 = 55). `marquee_competitive_goals_v_martj42` fails on Villa v Latvia and Griezmann v Netherlands, and `corrections_applied` fails on `unknown kind cap_goals`; both clear with the `cap_goals` handler (dry run: 13 pass, 0 fail, 1 pending).
